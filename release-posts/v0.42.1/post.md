## moai v0.42.1
### What's new
- **Pictures in issues and the wiki** — `moai image add` stores a picture (compressed by default, `--original` keeps it) and can append it to an issue; the web shows it in the detail and wiki, terminals that can draw pictures show it in `moai show`, and the explorer gives a placeholder with a clickable link
### Fixes
- v0.42.0 was tagged but never built for every platform; v0.42.1 is the first release with pictures

Release notes: <https://github.com/buzzni/moai/releases/tag/v0.42.1>
Update with `moai update` · install: <https://moai.buzzni.com/guide/#install>
