## moai v0.50.0
### What's new
- The web list and kanban fill unused space below their content with a subtle hatch, including when groups are folded
### Fixes
- Rule 5 takeover suggestions name every affected owner and preserve all requested IDs
- `[supervise] extra_lines` accepts TOML arrays, including multiline arrays, literal strings, trailing commas, and comments
