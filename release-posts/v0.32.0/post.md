## moai v0.32.0
### What's new
- **Gantt plans include rows with only a deadline** — they get a filled bar too
- **The TUI detail shows history and a work log**
- **TUI board titles wrap within each column**, up to 50 characters
- Web details can sit on the right or top as well as the left or bottom
- **Web Gantt groups fold independently of the list**
- Filled Gantt starts are written as absolute dates when you move them
- …and more in the release notes
### Fixes
- **TUI Gantt movement stays responsive on large trackers**
- Web history and work log preserve line breaks and wrap long text
