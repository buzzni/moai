## moai v0.26.0
### What's new
- **`moai wiki search <words…>`** finds the wiki's sections for some words and lists them best first, each with the `path:line` to open
- **`moai show <id>` shows the wiki about that issue** above its history
- **`moai wiki learn '<lesson>'`** writes a lesson page under `lessons/` and notes it on the issue it came from, in one call
- **Each lesson shows where it was met** — `×N` in `moai wiki ls`, a `Seen in` list in `moai wiki show`
- **The texts agents read now say when to write a lesson and how to read one**
