Different vendors. One team.

Agents from different vendors work together as a supervisor and its workers. The supervisor hands the remaining work to whichever workers are idle, so several tasks move at once. Start as many workers as you need: each one works in its own worktree, so nobody steps on anyone else, and the supervisor keeps things moving until the work is done.

  • One sentence is enough: Hand the backlog out to the idle sessions.
  • Clearing a worker's window, sending the next task, checking the report: the supervisor does all of it.
  • Planning to Claude Fable, development to Codex Sol, chores to Antigravity Gemini, review to Claude Opus. Give each job to the model that does it best.
